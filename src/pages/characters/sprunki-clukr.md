# Sprunki Silver (Clukr): The Cymbal-Wielding Innovator
Unveiling the Silver Sprunki: Clukr's Role in the Music Mod Clukr, the third character in the Incredibox mod Sprunki, is a silver-hued enigma that brings a unique percussive element to the musical ensemble through his signature cymbal sounds. Distinguished by his Samovar Silver color scheme, Shine White highlights, and Spanish Grey shadings, Clukr represents one of the more intellectually gifted members of the Sprunki universe 
.

## Physical Appearance and Design
Clukr's most notable feature is the silver cymbal mounted atop his head, complemented by an antenna with two hooks at its end. His default pose shows him gazing upward at his cymbal, creating a sense of perpetual fascination with his instrument 
.

## Musical Contribution
Clukr's primary contribution to the Sprunki soundscape comes through his cymbal/ride percussion sounds, created using the FLEX Plugin with a Jazz Kit Ride configuration. During performance, the cymbal on his head subtly bounces to the rhythm, while he maintains his characteristic upward gaze with a slightly parted mouth 
.

## Creative Legacy
Perhaps most significantly, Clukr is renowned for his technological achievements. Working alongside Garnold, he co-created two notable characters in the Sprunki universe: Fun Bot and Mr. Fun Computer. This creative partnership highlights his intellectual capabilities and innovative spirit, with building things being one of his primary hobbies 
.

## Horror Transformation
In his horror form, Clukr undergoes a dramatic transformation. The top half of his head is missing, though initially concealed by his cymbal. During his performance loops, this grim reality becomes apparent with bloodshot eyes, blood dripping from his head's interior and mouth, and his brain notably absent. The cymbal's movement becomes violently aggressive, repeatedly bashing against what remains of his head 
.

## Technical Details
An interesting technical aspect of Clukr's design is that his sound loops in normal mode synchronize with the countdown on the reset button, a feature he shares with Sky (though Sky demonstrates this in horror mode instead) 
.

## Personality and Relationships
Clukr, along with Garnold, are highly intelligent characters, being the creators of Fun Bot and Mr. Fun Computer. They are not related but are good friends 
.

## Trivia and fun Facts
Clukr's sound loops in normal mode match the countdown on the reset button, similar to Sky's feature in horror mode 
.
The extended length of his antenna in horror form has led to speculation about its true nature - possibly extending through his entire head or even his full body 
.

.

.
In Horror mode, before completely "dying", Clukr is most likely blind, due to his bloodshot eyes, similar to Pinki

.
## Conclusion: Clukr's Impact on Sprunki
Red Raddy's presence in Sprunki is more than just a beat; he's a character that adds depth and personality to the game. His unique audio signature, combined with his engaging personality and relationships, make him a beloved character among players. Whether you're a fan of his normal or horror version, Clukr's presence is integral to the Sprunki experience.
