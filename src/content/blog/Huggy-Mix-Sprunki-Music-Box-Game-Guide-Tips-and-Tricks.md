## Huggy Mix Sprunki Music Box Tips and Tricks

### Tip 1 Build From the Bottom Up

Always start with rhythm. A strong kick and snare pattern makes even a simple mix feel professional. Add melody only after your groove is locked.

### Tip 2 Use Silence as a Tool

Don't feel you need every slot filled. Removing a layer for a few beats creates contrast and makes the next drop hit harder. This is a classic sprunki music box trick that seasoned players use constantly.

### Tip 3 Experiment With the Huggy Characters

The Huggy-inspired characters in this edition are not just cosmetic. They often control the creepiest and most memorable sound layers. Rotate them in and out to find hidden combos.

### Tip 4 Watch the Character Animations

The visuals are a clue. When characters react strongly to the music, you're on the right track. Dull animations usually mean your mix needs more energy.

### Tip 5 Practice Daily for Speed

The more you play, the faster you recognise which sound fits where. Speed matters if you want to build complex mixes before the tempo loops finish.
