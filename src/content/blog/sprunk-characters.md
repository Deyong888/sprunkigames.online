At the core of Sprunki's music are the beat-makers, the characters who set the pace and rhythm for the compositions.

- **[Sprunki Silver (Clukr)](/characters/sprunki-clukr)** is a silver-hued enigma that brings a unique percussive element to the musical ensemble through his signature cymbal sounds.
- **[Sprunki Fun Bot](/characters/sprunki-funbot)** is the rhythmic soul of Sprunki, masterfully controlling the beat.
