## Huggy Mix Sprunki Music Box Tips and Tricks

Here are the strategies that experienced players use to get noticeably better results:

- **Do not fill every slot at once.** Empty space is a musical tool. A track with a few well-chosen loops usually sounds better than a chaotic wall of sound.
- **Remove and replace characters in real time.** You can swap a character mid-playback to hear how the mix changes instantly — this is the fastest way to find a better combination.
- **Start muted then build up.** Some players prefer to build a full track and then remove layers one by one to find the cleanest version.
- **Take note of character order.** Placement order can affect how loops sync together, so try dragging characters in different sequences.
